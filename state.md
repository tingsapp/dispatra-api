## Boundaries

Inbound email intake, AI agents, automatic dispatch, Stripe collection, payroll, remote push delivery are not implemented in this manual phase. Android foreground location and a bounded encrypted driver action/evidence queue are implemented in the separate driver app; see its state file for verification limits. Road provider responses are not persistently cached. Evidence images are stored in PostgreSQL pending object-storage and retention work. Completion queues Invoice email; one authorized recipient delivery was verified as recorded above. Continuous sending requires the email worker, and payment is separate. Partial quantity or failed delivery remains an issue requiring dispatcher resolution; full recovery/rebooking is not implemented.

The original client layouts are retained and now use these operational endpoints in company workspaces and role portals. `/prototype` is the separate local simulation. Browser demo records are not imported into PostgreSQL automatically.
